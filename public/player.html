How to play

Speed Quiz is a real-time, room-based multiplayer quiz game. Join with your room code and display name, then wait in the lobby until the host starts. The lobby shows the host, connected players, disconnected participants, and each player’s identifying colour. Answer questions as quickly as possible, follow the live leaderboard, view the final results, and replay in the same room.
